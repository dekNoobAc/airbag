Polymarket gave Bitcoin a 68% chance of closing below $74k. Odds don't protect you, though.

I built Airbag for NEAR HACKATHON: insurance on what Polymarket predicts. We crash-tested it live. 🧵

2/
Buy cover on an event, e.g. "pay me 100 USDC if BTC closes below $74k".

An AMM on NEAR sets the price, and it's never cheaper than Polymarket's odds. Every policy is backed 1:1 in USDC by underwriters.

3/
The odds come from an oracle running in a TEE. The contract pins its code hash, so there's no oracle key to steal: only that exact code can answer.

4/
Like a hook on Polymarket: every policy is mirrored as real YES + NO shares on Polymarket's own contracts on Polygon.

The NEAR contract builds those Polygon transactions itself, and NEAR's MPC network signs them. No private key exists anywhere.

5/
When Polymarket resolves, a dispute window runs, then every policy pays out automatically in USDC on NEAR or Base. No claim form.
